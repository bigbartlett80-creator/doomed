About the Wolf

Meet the Wolf. Stephen made him by hand: clay, silver ball joints, claws and all. He showed up in pieces, and that's the whole point. He's what a person look like when the people they love fall off. Cold, scattered, kinda cold-blooded about it.

But he ain't stay broke. His thesis is restoration: pick the pieces up, link back with your people, and get back to shinin'. Every time you reach out and reconnect with somebody, he snaps a little more back together. Get everybody to We Good and you'll see him dance.

“Back Together” — the Wolf’s theme

Hook Broke in pieces, but I ain't stay down, Snap it back together, wolf back in town. Called up my people, said it's been too long, Now the crew back shinin', that's the restoration song. Verse Cold world had me scattered on the floor, Silver in my joints, but I'm reachin' out once more. Ghost no more, I'm pickin' up the phone, Stars don't shine alone, we was built to glow.

Original music, synthesized live in your browser: a 98 BPM funk/hip‑hop groove for the dance and a quiet minor‑key ambient loop (“Cold Front”) for when he’s broken. Sound is off until you turn it on.
